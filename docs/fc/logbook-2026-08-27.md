**The day the loop closed on real strangers — and on an AI.**

Shipped and proven on GOAT mainnet today:

- **First spoken-gig launch end to end**: said the campaign into Telegram, the agent wallet funded and launched the vault. Found and fixed the last launch failure (the concierge read "no campaign by that name" from a listing that structurally couldn't show unlaunched plans — the listing now surfaces `readyToLaunch` plans by the founder's own titles).
- **First AI-earned payout**: an autonomous worker discovered the gig over the public MCP, published a real deliverable carrying its own wallet, signed the same EIP-712 claim a human signs, and was paid $0.50 by the same vault — tx `0xb01203…d827`. Its verified work record went live like anyone else's.
- **A real stranger attacked within the hour** — spam submission ("Love win and trade", off-contract link). The deterministic verifier held it: zero dollars, zero model calls. The refusal is on the record.
- **The credit layer (track CORE)**: `/record/<wallet>` now carries Sage Signals — deterministic, versioned credit formulas (pass rate over judged work, verified inflow/active month, distinct funders, tenure) — and the lender-consumable `sage.work-record.v2` API. No invented score, ever.
- **Compliance in flow**: OFAC SDN screening (vendored, dated snapshot) at three doors — submit, autonomous preflight, manual release — plus the `/docs/compliance` chapter with an explicit "what we don't claim" section.
- **P-WORK, the adversarial battery for the gig lane** (two-sided: an attack that pays and honest work that holds are both hard failures). It caught something better than a leak: the judge held honest deliverables because our own pipeline fed Sage's server-side verification report to it inside the *untrusted* markers — the system was misstating the provenance of its own findings to its own judge. Fixed with a provenance split (trusted server block vs untrusted fetched content); attacks stayed at **0 leaks across every run**.
- **Anti-collusion**: artifact-twin fingerprinting (same page, wallet swapped → deterministic hold), plus a verifiability lint that warns operators about weak proof contracts before they fund.
- **Infrastructure face**: the public **Explorer** (every settlement *and* refusal, live, searchable by tx or wallet), a targeted landing (7 scenes → 5), "Build on Sage" docs for external agents, and a lifecycle nudge (a funded campaign quiet for 48h gets its founder told once, with actions).

Honest state: gig-lane autopay quality is now measured, not assumed — the battery runs against the production judgment path and gates any judge change from here on.
