# Do not say

- **"Credit score."** Sage computes signals; the lender picks the multiple. Bankers will catch this.
- **"We're a bank"** or anything that implies Sage holds local currency. It never does. Licensed partners do.
- **A claim link, on screen or aloud.** They are bearer money. Whoever sees one can take it.
- **"She"** for the seller, after you've said you played the seller. Use "the recipient".
