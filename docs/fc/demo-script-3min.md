# The 3-minute demo — facecam + screen, read and do

Rules for the take: one browser window, tabs already open in this order (see "Set up" below). Speak to
the camera for the first and last lines; everything else is voice over the screen. Where a wait would
happen (funding confirmation, the agent verifying), you pause the recording and cut. Nobody needs to
watch a spinner. Target 2:50. If a step goes wrong, keep talking and cut it later.

Every number you say is on the public ledger. Say "yesterday" for the grant that already ran; it did.

## The script

Left is what you say. Right is what your hands do. Times are where you should be, not a stopwatch.

### 0:00 · Camera · the problem
**Say:** "Hi, I'm Shariq. This is Sage. Across the Caribbean, four in five businesses are small, and most of them can't borrow, because nothing trustworthy records that they're good for it. Sage fixes that from the other end. It pays people for verified work, and the paperwork writes itself."

**Do:** Look at the camera. Then switch to the screen.

### 0:20 · Tab 1 · say the work once
**Say:** "You describe the work once, in your own currency. Here's a grant to a market seller in Kingston. Sixteen hundred Jamaican dollars, two milestones."

**Do:** Paste the sentence. Wait for the draft.

**Say:** "Sage reads it and drafts the brief. What counts as done, what evidence it will check, and the money, split equally. It priced the milestones in J-dollars at today's rate. No model ever sets an amount. And I tick 'only people I invite', so this never goes on a public board."

**Do:** Scroll the draft slowly. Point at the J$ line and the rate. Tick **Only people I invite.** Click **Create the grant**.

**Say:** "I fund it once, from my own wallet. From here Sage spends without me, and it can't spend past this."

**Do:** Click fund, confirm in the wallet. **Cut the wait.** Come back when it says live.

### 0:55 · Tab 2 · the seller's side
**Say:** "The seller needs no bank and no app. She gets one link on WhatsApp, opens it, and pastes the page she built. This one ran yesterday. Let me show you what happened."

**Do:** Show the campaign page with the two milestones marked paid. Hover the WhatsApp share once.

### 1:05 · Tab 3 · the receipt
**Say:** "Sage opened her page itself, checked every line of the brief, and paid. Fifty-eight seconds from submit to paid. Eight hundred J-dollars, five dollars five, at the stamped rate. Here's the transaction, and here's why it paid, with the quote it relied on."

**Do:** Scroll: the amount, the J$ line, the transaction, then down to "Why Sage paid". Pause on the quote.

**Say:** "The receipt is public. Her balance isn't. On Starknet the money sits behind a commitment and she collects it into a private note. Anyone can check the payment. Nobody can see her savings."

**Do:** Point at "The private leg", then "Collected".

### 1:35 · Tab 4 · the record
**Say:** "Every payout lands on her record. Verified inflow, pass rate, how many different people paid her. Published formulas over receipts. Sage doesn't score anyone."

**Do:** Scroll the signals. Point at "100% of 3 judged".

**Say:** "And here's the part banks will care about. Twelve minutes after her first payout, she took an advance against that inflow. One click, no application. Her next payout repaid it automatically. Zero outstanding."

**Do:** Scroll to the advance facility. Point at "$1.85 advance · repaid from 1 verified payout".

### 2:05 · Tab 5 and 6 · the paper a bank asks for
**Say:** "She can print a verified income statement. Every row is a transaction a loan officer can check on chain."

**Do:** Click **Print a verified income statement**. Show the print preview for two seconds. Cancel.

**Say:** "And a lender gets the same thing as an API call. Capacity is arithmetic they can see, not a score they have to trust."

**Do:** Tab 6. Point at the multiple and the capacity line.

### 2:25 · Tab 7 · it says no
**Say:** "This only works because the agent refuses. Twenty-six of the last sixty-five decisions were refusals, each with the reason on record. A rail that pays everything is a leak."

**Do:** Scroll the explorer to a refusal. Point at the reason.

### 2:38 · Tab 8 · what's next
**Say:** "Live today: two settlement rails, fourteen currencies with J-dollars first, one person one slot, sanctions screening on every payout. Next, with licensed partners: fund from a bank account, cash out in local currency, and a WhatsApp door."

**Do:** Scroll the roadmap once, slowly.

### 2:50 · Camera · the ask
**Say:** "We built this for the cooperative paying sellers every week, the programme funding forty businesses in three currencies, and the lender who'd rather price cash flow than collateral. Everything you saw is on the public ledger. Come run one with us."

**Do:** Camera. Stop.

## If you need to cut 20 seconds
Drop the explorer beat (2:25). The refusal number is on the Caribbean page's controls section anyway.

## Do not say
- "Sage scores" or "credit score". Sage computes signals; the lender sets the multiple.
- "Bank" for what Sage is. Sage never holds local currency; licensed partners do.
- Any claim link, ever. They are bearer money.
