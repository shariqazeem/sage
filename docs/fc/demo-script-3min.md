## Yes, switch tabs — and here's why

Pre-load all nine tabs and move **left to right**. Never type a URL on camera.

- A fresh page load costs 2 to 4 seconds. Nine of them is thirty seconds of watching nothing.
- Left to right is the path a real visitor takes, so it reads as exploring, not as a slideshow.
- A loaded tab cannot fail. A live load can.
- The **only** thing that must happen live is the composer. That is the part people need to see happening.

- **The WhatsApp hover** — nice, but it does not survive being on screen for two seconds.
- **Testing and gigs** get covered in beat 3 where the buttons are literally on screen, not as their own tab.

# What you never say

- **Nothing about who the recipient was.** The script never claims a person got paid, and it doesn't
  need to. It says "here's one that ran, both milestones verified and paid" — the system's behaviour,
  which is exactly what you're demonstrating. Your real-users claim is the aggregate on tab 1: forty-one
  payouts to twenty-four people. That's true, it's on screen, and it's stronger than one story.
- **"Credit score."** Sage computes signals. The lender picks the multiple. Bankers will catch this.
- **"We're a bank"**, or anything implying Sage holds local currency. It never does. Partners do.
- **A claim link**, on screen or aloud. Whoever sees one can take the money.
