## What the rubric actually scores (futurecaribbean.com/judging-rubric)

Two halves, 1–10 each. **Business strength (50%)** = average of team quality, product innovation
and defensibility, product-market fit. **Agentic AI excellence (50%)** = sophistication of the AI
architecture, autonomous agents, multi-agent coordination, workflow orchestration, reasoning,
human-in-the-loop design, compute efficiency, implementation quality, scalability, real-world
impact. No Caribbean-team bonus, no AI screening stated.

The track's bar: "we are not looking for apps, we are looking for systems that move capital" —
lower fees, near-real-time settlement, collateral-free MSME credit, diaspora capital into
investment, regional capital movement — and solutions "must work with publicly available datasets".
