# The last three days — one plan

*17 → 21 Aug 2026. Written to be followed, not admired.*

**The single goal: one external founder funds their own campaign.**

Everything else is secondary. It is the one activation event our own Growth Metrics Proposal named
and the one number still at zero. It also happens to be the thing the product needs to exist without
us paying for it.

**The constraint:** ~$12 USDC left and no model credits. So this plan spends **words and existing
assets**, not money.

---

## What changed today, and why it matters to this plan

| Change | Why it unlocks the goal |
|---|---|
| Wallet required to inspect | Cost is bounded and every inspection has an owner we can follow up with |
| Tester supply proof on `/launch` and every plan page | Answers the founder's real objection at the moment they decide |
| Landing CTA points at `/marketplace` | Testers stop landing on a dead campaign |

The supply proof is the important one. A founder does not hesitate over $5 — they hesitate because
**funding a campaign nobody answers is embarrassing.** They can now see: 15 people paid, $47.55
settled, typical payout under three minutes, 45% of submissions refused.

---

## Day 1 (today) — go back to the four who already asked

You have their products and, for most, a free inspection already paid for. Do **not** pitch Sage.
Send them their plan.

**The message — one per founder, personalised in the first line only:**

> Hey — I ran Sage on [their product] and here's the plan it came back with: [link]
>
> It found [one specific thing from their plan]. Costs you nothing so far; the inspection is on me.
>
> If you want real people to actually run these missions, it's **$5** — that funds 3 testers. 15
> people have been paid on Sage this week, typical payout under 3 minutes, and 45% of submissions
> get refused, so you're paying for verified work rather than attendance.
>
> Want me to set it up?

**The list:** Areej / ClawUp · Zainab / TokenWatcher · Aporix · Triage · the hackathon-group founder
who asked how to get started.

**Why this converts and the old approach didn't:** you're not asking them to imagine anything. The
plan exists, the testers exist, the price is five dollars.

is the other side.

>

## What NOT to do

- **Don't fund another campaign yourself.** One external founder funding $5 is worth more to the
  report, the panel and the product than another campaign you paid for.
- **Don't buy more model credits to chase volume.** Inspections are now wallet-gated; spend what's
  left on founders who might convert, not on traffic that won't.
- **Don't start new features.** Three days. The product works; the gap is distribution.
- **Don't chase the two open bugs** (product-name misread, the 20-minute hang). Real, logged, not
  worth the last three days.
