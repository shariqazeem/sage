# Sage — user feedback log

Everything real users told us during Stage 2, verbatim, from **both** channels feedback arrives on.

---

## Why there are two sections

We first recorded "paid testers give no feedback" as a finding: four ClawUp testers were paid and
none replied when asked for feedback afterwards. That reading was wrong. Their feedback was inside
their submissions the whole time — we had simply built no screen that showed a founder what the
people they paid had written, and mistook that silence for absence.

So this log carries both:

1. **Mission reports** — what testers wrote when completing paid work. This is the substantive
   channel, and it is verified: Sage checks each account against what it observed in the product.
2. **Feedback widget** — unprompted notes anyone can leave. Unverified by design, and it shows.

**22 mission reports · 15 widget submissions** · compiled 17 Aug 2026
