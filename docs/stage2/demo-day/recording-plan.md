## Before you hit record

1. **Pick a product Sage has never inspected.** Fresh matters — a repeat inspection looks staged.
   Something with a real signup or a core action, not a one-page site.
2. **Fund the vault in advance** and stop the recording there. Wallet confirmations are slow and
   dull; nobody needs to watch a MetaMask popup for forty seconds.
3. **Have a second wallet ready** for the refused submission.
4. **Open the GOAT explorer in a tab** so the closing verification is one click, not a search.
5. Close every other tab. Full screen. 1080p or better.

## The shot list — 1:45

### Shot 1 · Point it at the product (0:00–0:20 of the recording)

Paste the URL, pick the goal, hit continue. **Then say nothing and let the browsing play.**

> *"I've never pointed it at this before. It's opening the product in a real browser right now —
> those are pages it's actually visiting."*

The exploration is the most visually convincing thing Sage does. Do not talk over all of it.

### Shot 2 · The missions it wrote (0:20–0:45)

Scroll the plan slowly. Land on one mission and read the title out loud.

> *"I didn't write any of this. It wrote these missions from what it just saw — this one exists
> because it found that flow, not because I told it to look."*

Point at the budget split: total, allocated, per-mission reward, completions.

> *"And it's already divided the budget. I never picked those numbers."*

### Shot 3 · A real person gets paid (0:45–1:15)

Submit genuine work from wallet one. Then **stay quiet and let the payout land.**

> *"That's a real submission. It's checking that account against what it saw itself..."*

When it settles, open the receipt.

> *"Paid. Real USDC, on GOAT mainnet. That's the transaction hash — and this receipt page shows
> the exact evidence it paid for."*

### Shot 4 · Now try to cheat it (1:15–1:40) — **this is the shot that wins**

Second wallet. Submit something thin — a vague sentence that could have been written without
opening the product. Say plainly that you are trying to get paid without doing the work.

> *"Now let me try to cheat it. I'm going to submit something that sounds plausible but I never
> actually did the task."*

Let the refusal come back and read the reason aloud.

> *"Refused. And it tells me why. I built this thing and it just said no to me."*

### Shot 5 · Verify it (1:40–1:45)

Cut to the GOAT explorer, already open on the transaction.

> *"That's the block explorer. Not my dashboard — the chain. Every number in this demo is there."*

## Rules for the voiceover

- **Do not narrate clicks.** "Now I'll click continue" is dead air. Say what it *means*.
- **Let the silences run.** The browsing and the payout are the product. Talking over them buries it.
- **Never say a competitor's name**, and never imply anyone else's numbers aren't real. Say
  *"this is a transaction you can check yourself"* and let the comparison make itself.
- **Say "real" only when it's load-bearing.** Once about the browser, once about the money.
- If a take goes wrong, start that shot again. It is pre-recorded — there is no reason to ship a
  bad one.

If the video only lands one sentence, it should be this:

> whole difficulty.**

It's on slide 6, and Shot 4 is the proof of it.
