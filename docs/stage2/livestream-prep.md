## Q3 · Hardest part

Pick **one**. Don't do all three.

**The RPC one (best story):**
- a node froze for 10 hours and still said it was healthy
- our server believed a chain from 10 hours ago
- real transactions were invisible to us
- **"a broken node is loud. A lying node is quiet."**

**The money one (best if they push on safety):**
- the hard part isn't paying, it's deciding
- if the model decides, a jailbreak costs you real money
- so the model never touches the amount — it proposes, the contract disposes
- **"the AI proposes, the vault disposes."**

**The one about testing (if the talk goes technical):**
- our best planner was broken for 3 days, everything looked green
- model was returning a perfect answer wrapped in a code fence, our parser refused it
- found it by reading production, not tests
- **"tests tell you the code does what you wrote. Only production tells you what users got."**

## Q4 · Real users — SLOW DOWN HERE, this is your segment

**What surprised you:** *strangers do careful work for $2.50.*

- 4 testers, same product, never saw each other's work
- all reproduced the same exact pairing command, same credential map
- two pasted their agent's reply — completely different replies
- that's also how the copy-detection cleared them

**The killer stat — say this slowly:**

> "Ten got paid, three got held. The paid ones wrote 293 to 1,457 characters. The held ones wrote 35 to 142. **No human made any of those calls.**"

**What users changed:**
- two hit a bug that killed whole inspections → fixed same day
- one caught Sage misreading a product's name
- one used it on iPhone — surface we'd never tested

**Say the honest gap out loud before they ask:**
> "Everything so far, I funded. Whether founders pay for it themselves — that's what I still have to prove."

## Q6 · What's missing in agents

**Frame:** *agents can't be trusted with money because the safety is written in prompts. A prompt is a suggestion.*

- what made me comfortable: limits live in a contract the agent doesn't control
- it can ask for anything, the vault just refuses
- **"everyone's building agents that can act. Almost nobody's building what decides what they're allowed to do when nobody's watching."**

**If asked why most agent demos feel fake:**
> "There's no counterparty. Sage works because on the other side there's a person who wants to get paid. That demand is way easier to find than an agent that wants to buy something from another agent."
