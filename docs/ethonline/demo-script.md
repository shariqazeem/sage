# Demo script — Sage on Arc

**A fresh email, a real faucet, a real payout. Nothing pre-made.**

Read the lines as you do the action above them. Say them in your own words if that's easier.
Target is **3 minutes 10**, and it fits in 2 to 4 either way. Cut points are marked if you
run long.

---

## Before you press record

- Window one: sagepays.xyz, **signed out**, 1440 wide
- Window two: a private window, a worker wallet already funded with a little Arc USDC
- Tab ready but not open: `faucet.circle.com`
- Clipboard: `Publish a public page containing the text SAGE-ARC-2026 and submit its URL. $1, one slot.`
- A fresh email you can read codes from, open in another tab
- Camera on for beat 1 and beat 8 only

**One thing that will break the take if you skip it.** When you post the work, tick **"Only
people I invite."** A public campaign asks every worker to prove they're one person with
World ID first, which is a real feature and a great one, but it costs a minute you don't
have in a three-minute demo, and no EVM wallet on prod has been through it yet. Invite-only
skips that door and is honest: you're paying someone you know.

## Running long? Cut in this order

1. The mandate line in beat 3. Say only "it runs under a policy Sage can't widen."
2. The second half of beat 4. Let the balance land silently.
3. Beat 7 down to one sentence: "every payout leaves a receipt anyone can open."

## If something stalls

- **Faucet is slow or asks for a captcha.** Keep talking, do it, and if it drags past fifteen
  seconds say "it takes a moment" and cut the gap in the edit.
- **The judge takes its time.** Talk over it. "It's fetching the page and checking the text."
  A real wait beats a jump cut.
- **A launch fails.** Switch to a campaign from earlier this week and say so. Every receipt
  is real either way.
- **The worker is asked to verify with World ID.** You left the campaign public. Stop the
  take, repost it with "Only people I invite" ticked.
- **You fumble.** Stop, breathe, say the line again. Fix it in the edit. Never restart the
  whole take.
