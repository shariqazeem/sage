Most of the work inside a company is not a job posting. It's "can you get the catalogue page up by Friday" — and then the awkward part: proving it's done, and paying for it.

Sage now does that inside your company.

Compose a gig or a milestone grant. Tick one box: Only people I invite. The campaign never appears on the public marketplace. You get a link — share it with your team, your contractors, your grantees.

They open the link, do the work, submit. Sage checks the deliverable itself — the page is live, the phrase is on it, the transaction happened — and the vault releases the money. Same judge, same rules, same receipt as every other payout on Sage.

What makes it different from a payroll tool: there's no HR system, no invoice, no bank account needed on their side. And on the private rail, a team's pay is neither a public ledger nor a public listing.

Every payout still lands on the person's own verified work record — the one a lender can underwrite. Getting paid by your employer through Sage builds the same credit file as getting paid by a stranger.
