## Why this shape

The first line is the whole product in one sentence and contains the two words that make people stop:
*decided* and *paid*. The refusal count is second because it is the only thing that makes the payout
count mean anything — anyone can pay 39 people; refusing 26 is the claim.

The farm is stated as a WIN, not hidden. It is the most credible thing we have: we were attacked, we
caught it on-chain, we published the picture, and we changed the incentive so it cannot pay next time.
