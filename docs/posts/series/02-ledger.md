# Post 2 — the ledger (video: `videos/02-ledger.mp4`, 21s)

An AI that pays people should be checkable by anyone, not trusted by anyone.

So Sage keeps a public ledger: every settlement and every refusal, each anchored to an on-chain transaction, across both rails. Open any row and you get the receipt — what the agent saw, what it replayed, why it paid or held.

Refusals are listed with the same weight as payments. That is what makes the payments mean something.

Every number on our site — the landing, the outcomes page, the marketplace — is computed from this one ledger as the page renders. Nothing is typed in.
