# Post 5 — Sage for teams (video: `videos/05-teams.mp4`, 20s)

Most of the work inside a company is not a job posting. It's "can you get the catalogue page up by Friday" — and then the awkward part: proving it's done, and paying for it.

Sage now does that inside your company. Compose a gig or a milestone grant, tick one box — Only people I invite — and the campaign never appears on the public marketplace. You get a link. Share it with your team, your contractors, your grantees.

They open the link, do the work, submit. Sage checks the deliverable itself and the vault releases the money. Same judge, same rules, same receipt as every other payout.

No HR system, no invoice, no bank account needed on their side. On the private rail, a team's pay is neither a public ledger nor a public listing. And every payout still builds the person's own verified record.
